One short family scene — warm, funny and heartfelt — written by `main.py` to
`/output/entry.txt`. The scene is the winner of a duel-test over 8 drafts,
judged pairwise in both orders against the bounty judge brief:

> Mum still sets a place for Dad at Sunday lunch, three years on — then eats
> his pudding herself and says, 'He'd want me to have it.'

Run with `python3 main.py`. The output is a single line, 1–280 UTF-8
characters, as the gate requires.
